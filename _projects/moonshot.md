In Moonshot, you take control of a roller coaster, with jumps, tricks, and progressively unlocked
power-ups to reach further and further tracks. Be quick on track switches to be able to climb to
difficult areas, and fast on the combo buttons, or you'll never build up enough speed. As we built
it we found our love for classic flash games of our youth really coming out in the design. See if
you can make it ***all the way to the moon***.

This was my biggest test in level design, with basically the entire continuous world designed by me,
along with all the tuning and pacing of unlocks to keep it challenging enough the whole way.
